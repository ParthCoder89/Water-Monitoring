Project Overview

Explore the problem, our smart solution, system workflow, live dashboard, future vision and technology.

Problem

Water Scarcity & Tank Overflow

Many homes and communities waste thousands of liters of water due to unnoticed tank overflow, dry running motors, and the absence of real-time water level monitoring.

Read More →

Solution

NeerPilot

An intelligent IoT-based system that continuously monitors water levels, automates motor control and helps prevent water wastage with real-time alerts & remote access.

Read More →

Working

Simple & Intelligent Workflow

Ultrasonic sensors collect water level data, ESP8266 processes the information, and the dashboard displays live updates while controlling the motor automatically.
