About Our Devices

NeerPilot consists of two separate hardware units — the Tank Unit and the Home Unit — working together over a wireless link to give you reliable, real-time water monitoring and control.

System Overview

How the System Works Together

NeerPilot's two units communicate wirelessly: the Tank Unit senses and transmits, the cloud relays, and the Home Unit displays and responds — a simple, reliable loop that keeps you informed without any manual effort.

Tank Unit

Sensing and Control at the Source

The Tank Unit is the sensing and control heart of NeerPilot, mounted directly at the water tank. It measures water level, manages power and safely controls the motor.

Important: NeerPilot does not use an ultrasonic sensor for water level detection. Instead, it uses a Water Level Wire Sensor — a set of stainless steel sensing wires placed at different heights inside the tank, which detect water contact to determine the level with high reliability.

Components

Working Principle

Water Touches Sensing Wires

Rising or falling water contacts wires set at different heights.

ESP32 Detects Active Wire

The controller reads exactly which wires are in contact with water.

Calculate Percentage

Wire positions are converted into an accurate fill percentage.

Transmit Data

Readings are sent wirelessly to the cloud and Home Unit.

Home Unit Receives

The Home Unit picks up the latest status the moment it arrives.

Dashboard Updates

Protects the motor with live voltage and current awareness.
